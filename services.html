Our services

A go-to-market plan that fits the hold period.

Private equity firms buy companies with a clock running. Keystone GTM Partners is a go-to-market advisory built around that clock. We work alongside portfolio-company leadership to find the two or three revenue levers that matter most, put a plan around them within weeks, and measure progress in numbers an investment committee and a future buyer will trust. Our work is hands-on: we sit with sales, marketing and customer teams, not just in the boardroom.

Three ways we help

Focused on what moves revenue.

Positioning & pricing

We interview customers and lost deals, sharpen the ideal customer profile, rewrite the value story and test pricing and packaging so the company wins more of the right deals at better margins.

Pipeline & sales engine

We design the lead-to-close process, set up outbound and partner channels, install a simple operating cadence and dashboards, and coach the team so pipeline becomes predictable instead of heroic.

Exit-ready growth story

We turn results into the metrics buyers care about: net revenue retention, sales efficiency, payback and pipeline coverage, packaged into a clear, defensible narrative for diligence.

How an engagement runs

From diagnosis to results in 100 days.

Weeks 1–2: Diagnose

Review the funnel, customers, pricing and team. Agree the three highest-impact revenue levers with leadership and the deal team.

Weeks 3–6: Design

Rewrite positioning and offers, build the pipeline plan and set targets and weekly metrics for every lever.

Weeks 7–14: Execute

Launch campaigns and sales motions, coach the team and adjust weekly based on what the numbers say.

Ongoing: Prove it

Hand over a scorecard and playbook the company owns, plus a clean growth story ready for the next board meeting or buyer.
